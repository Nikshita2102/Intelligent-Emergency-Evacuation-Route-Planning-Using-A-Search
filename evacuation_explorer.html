How the planner thinks

  • State = (cell, time). Waiting is a move, so people can queue instead of colliding.
  • Fire is a known forecast. A cell is blocked from the moment it ignites, and cells about to burn cost extra.
  • Reservations. Each planned person books their cells at each time; later people route around those bookings.
  • Retry. Planning is in priority order, so someone can get boxed in. Failed people are moved to the front and everyone replans.
